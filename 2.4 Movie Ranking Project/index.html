My top 3 movies of all-time

1. The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (2003)

3 hours of scifi storytelling that will leave you breathless.

2. The Matrix (1999)

A philosophical sci-fi thriller that revolutionised action cinema.

3. Inception (2010)

A mind-bending heist film that explores the nature of dreams and reality.
